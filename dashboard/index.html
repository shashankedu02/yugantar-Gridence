How to read this

Input days
Three consecutive days of past weather (ERA5 reanalysis). The forecast starts the day after.
Baseline
The forecast from those days, unchanged.
Shock
A change you add to the last input day only, in real units.
Scenario
The forecast after the shock.
Difference
Scenario minus baseline. Orange is higher, blue is lower, dark is no change.
Grid cell
1° square, about 110 km. Each value is an average over the cell and the day.

What makes the forecast

LocalNet C64 is a small neural network that predicts the next day from the last three days, then repeats. It runs on this computer; no weather service is called.

DAP (damped anomaly persistence) is the fallback: a simple statistical rule used if LocalNet cannot run. The line above the map names the model that answered each forecast.

Impact indicators are computed in your browser from the forecast on screen. Each is a proxy with its method and limitation.

Limits you should know

  • Unvalidated: no second person has reproduced our test results.
  • A shock response is what this model does. It is not proof of what the atmosphere would do.
  • In local tests some shocks did not spread, and one produced an implausible rain change at one place.
  • Detail fades at longer lead times, especially in rain. Heavy rain is poorly predicted.
  • Not station rain, not daily maximum temperature, not a flood or heatwave warning.
  • The coastline and city names are reference geography. They do not make the 1° data any finer. No political boundaries are drawn.

How to use MonsoonTwin

MonsoonTwin forecasts India’s daily weather on a 1° grid, and lets you ask “what if today were different here?”

  1. Pick a weather case — three real past days.
  2. Choose how many days to forecast.
  3. Pick a shock, or none. The map shows where it applies before you run.
  4. Run. With a shock, two forecasts are made: without it (baseline) and with it (scenario).
  5. Explore: switch variables, move through the days or press Play, and open Difference.
  6. Inspect: click a cell for its chart, and read the impact indicators.

Keyboard (optional): ← → day · Space play/pause · 1–6 variable · B S D view. With the map focused, arrows move the selected cell.
